BEST AVERAGE RANK: MICE
RECOMMENDED METHOD: MICE

WHY:
- The features are almost uncorrelated, so KNN and MICE have little information to use. Simple methods (mean / median) perform about as well and are much faster.
- Mean/median keep the column centre but shrink the spread (std). Lower std = distorted distribution.
- Mode is a poor choice for continuous columns (many unique values) but fine for flags and categories.
- MICE usually keeps the distribution best when features are related, but it is the slowest.
- Final choice: MICE. Use median for skewed columns, mode for categorical/binary columns, and KNN/MICE when columns are related and the dataset is not huge.
- Caveat: this test assumes values are missing completely at random. If missingness depends on other values (MAR/MNAR), prefer KNN or MICE and add a 'was_missing' indicator column.
