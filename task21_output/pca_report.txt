- PCA can NOT compress this data well: 18 of 20 components are needed for 95%. The variance is spread almost evenly (PC1 = 8.4%; an even split is 5.0%), so the columns are mostly independent. PCA works best when columns are correlated.
- With 18 components the model scores 0.9625; with all 20 original features 0.9610.

HOW TO CHOOSE
- To keep the INFORMATION in the data: use the cumulative-variance rule (18 components for 95%).
- To keep the PREDICTIVE power: use the cross-validated rule. A component with little variance can still matter for the target, because PCA ignores the target.
- Kaiser and parallel analysis are statistical rules about structure; Kaiser tends to keep too many, parallel analysis is stricter.
- The scaler and PCA are fitted inside each cross-validation fold (no leakage).
- Disadvantage: components are mixtures of original columns, so they are harder to explain (see loadings.csv).
