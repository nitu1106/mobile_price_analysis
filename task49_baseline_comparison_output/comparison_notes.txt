BASELINE MODEL COMPARISON

1. Linear Regression is not naturally a classification algorithm. Its predictions were rounded to the nearest price category for baseline comparison.

2. Logistic Regression is interpretable through its coefficients and is suitable for classification.

3. Decision Tree is interpretable through its decision rules and feature splits.

4. Models were evaluated using accuracy, weighted precision, weighted recall, and weighted F1-score.

5. The model with the highest weighted F1-score performed best in this baseline comparison.
